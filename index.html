The lake

Big Clear Lake

A quiet lake in the hills of Central Frontenac. Swim off the dock or take the canoe along the shoreline.

Fishing

Bass, walleye, pike and perch. The lake association has restored a walleye spawning bed to keep the fishery healthy.

Bringing a boat? There's a public launch on Price Road, marked on the map.
